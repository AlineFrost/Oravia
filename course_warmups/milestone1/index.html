    Before moving on to Expansion, let's see how much you can do.

    Below is a conversation between Ana and Kai. They are planning a family meal by phone. This dialogue is more challenging and contains a few words you haven't learned yet, to mimic real language encounter. Try to understand from context first, then check the tips below after reading. You may understand more than you expect!

    1. Ana: Eon?

    2. Kai: Eon, Ana!
    Ce run i anye litamis?
    Nim fa i eodenbor e nim bo.

    3. Ana: Oila!

    4. Kai: ...Su a nim i fou i boemo u haya.

    5. Ana: Run i boemo?
    Nim i ilianum ca run i do i boemo!

    6. Kai: I doum.
    Caora nim i ilace e run.

    7. Ana: Ia, ia.
    Cei i anvu?

    8. Kai: Nim fare, nim fasu su hay falen.
    Par ilhei.

    9. Ana: Su haya i dapas e ce?

    10. Kai: I iliro e mocen,
    mai nim fasu i pasum e moyi.

    11. Ana: Eta ilace e run fare!
    Run i do i ilaluan u hay litam.

    12. Kai: I ilacear litamar.
    Hay i ilaluan ca "e ce run i anye, nim i dapas".

    13. Ana: Hmm... a noi daco a yugaium u run.

    14. Kai: Ia.
    Ilie e mogali su moaria molcui?

    15. Ana: Kai.
    A par ilhei i anvu en run bo,
    su run i anona e moaria?

    16. Kai: Ceora um?
    A moaria a yuba.

    17. Ana: Um, nim i do i boemo e elireva mo u haya.

    18. Kai: Eloan!
    Mai a nim boemo a yalgai.

    19. Ana: Eta runa i do i anifi en nim bo.
    Nim i gelna e mo, su i boemo.

    20. Kai: Oia, yuba!
    Nim i anona e gerina u run cali nim i do.

    21. Ana: Su celi haya i anifi?

    22. Kai: Litamis en alui litetan.
    Mai a nim fare a yamirli,
    eta hay i anvu ho yasoi.

    23. Ana: Mai i lupupi litamis, dou um?

    24. Kai: Nim i ilianum, mai i dami a fare beivu.
    Eloan!
    Eta en liperis, nim i boemo u run.

    25. Ana: Nima i ilaluan e ca lili en liperis!
    Bi oi ca nim i eodani e run fare.
    Hay i do i ilaluan e oimel de run,
    cali a run a falen.

    26. Kai: Oinesum.

    Answer Sheet

    1. True or False?

    a) Kai knows how to cook. UM / false

    b) Kai's sibling does not like sugar. IA / true

    c) They will eat at Ana's house tomorrow. IA / true

    2. Sequence

    b) Kai asks his parent (yesterday)

    a) Ana buys the food (before cooking for the parents tomorrow)

    d) Kai's family arrives (tomorrow, three o'clock)

    c) Kai cooks for Ana (next week)

    3. Open Questions

    a) Ceora a Kai i anyeum e mocen?

    Possible answer: Caora a Kai fasu i pasum e moyi.

    b) Cedom haya i mo litamis?

    Possible answer: Haya i mo en Ana bo, caora a Kai boemo a yalgai.

    Dialogue Translation

    Ana: Hello?

    Kai: Hello, Ana! What are you doing tomorrow? My family is visiting my house.

    Ana: Happy!

    Kai: ...And I need to cook for them.

    Ana: You cook? I did not know you could cook!

    Kai: I cannot. That is why I am asking you.

    Ana: Yes, yes. Who is coming?

    Kai: My parent, my sibling and their child. Three people.

    Ana: And what do they like?

    Kai: I was thinking chocolate, but my sibling does not like sugar.

    Ana: So ask your parent! You can talk to them today.

    Kai: I asked yesterday. They said "whatever you make, I will like."

    Ana: This answer is not easy for you.

    Kai: Right. Maybe coffee and a bowl of apples?

    Ana: Kai. Three people are coming to your house, and you give apples?

    Kai: Why not? Apples are good.

    Ana: No, I can cook real food for them.

    Kai: Thanks! But my kitchen is small.

    Ana: So you can all come to my house. I buy the food, and I cook.

    Kai: Yay, good! I will give you money when I can.

    Ana: And when are they coming?

    Kai: Tomorrow at five o'clock. But my parent is old, so they move slowly.

    Ana: But will it rain tomorrow, or not?

    Kai: I do not know, but there is my parent's car. Thanks! So next week, I cook for you.

    Ana: We will talk about that again next week! I am glad that I will meet your parent. They can tell stories about you from when you were a child.

    Kai: Not funny.
