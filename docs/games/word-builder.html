This plays like the real game: you start with 4 wood and nothing else, and can collect 10 new words a day in the meadow and 8 from Area 2 on (20 on the first day). Area 1 (meadow) has 91 words from Oravia in 12 Lessons: the first day brings the easiest ones, then the rest come in lesson order, except that words sharing a building block are kept on different days in the first days. Areas 2 to 5 have their full word lists (143, 214, 182 and 193 words), from the flashcard decks plus grammar words from the lessons and the grammar. Test tools: Collect 5 or 10 words skips the puzzle and counts toward today's new words (it can go past the limit), Do today's reviews answers all of today's review tasks correctly, Skip a day stands in for real days (reviews, seasons and events arrive the next day), and the material, coin and area buttons help you look around.
