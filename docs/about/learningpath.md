# Learn Oravia in 2 months!

Oravia was designed to be fast and easy to learn, and it has a structured learning path. The estimates are based on data from previous learners. It takes about 2 months of 30-45 minutes a day, with these instructions:

## Month 1

. Do 2 lessons a day. Start at the 10-Minute Grammar, then Lesson 0, up until you finish the course.

. Do 5 flashcards a day. The flashcard deck with spaced repetition follows the same vocabulary you see in the course. The extra practice will help the course be maximally effective.

## Month 2

. Do 10 flashcards a day. The flashcard deck with spaced repetition will cover more words that appeared in the course, plus the core list from the dictionary. By the end, you will know about 450 words, the most frequent ones.

. Practice. Practice has two aspects, which can be alternated by day. The first is passive, by doing things like reading The Little Prince in Oravia, or watching videos. The second is active, by doing things like writing your own texts or chatting with people on Discord.

That's it! By the end of 2 months, you will likely be able to communicate in Oravia, consume content, and read books. You will also know 95%+ of words that you encounter in the wild.


# Month 3: Road to B1

If you want to continue your studies, you can follow the Road to B1! Here's the daily path on month 3:

. Do 13 flashcards a day. By the end, you will know all 850 Oravia words.

. Practice. Practice has two aspects, which can be alternated by day. The first is passive, by doing things like reading books in Oravia, or watching videos. The second is active, by doing things like producing content and translations. Importantly, to be able to reach a conversational level, you need to practice actual spoken conversations, which can be done via audio chat on Discord.

. Optional: read the Comprehensive Grammar and the Guide to Craft & Style.

By the end of 3 months, you will likely be able to use the language creatively, engage with a wide range of content, and take the B1 test.
