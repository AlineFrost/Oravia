## Read

**O Jerih Coupa**

O tor ganter i eodani e hayjor, nim i siyal ca a eledora a eodyel.

Cali falen, nim i none e yunitam ciuseri de coupa. E noi coupa i anja sora eledora daelipo. 

Lar, o e coupa de jerih i anopu. Nim i copei su copei en mir lidastor, mai i doum i siyal.

Noi, noli, i dami. Hayjor i vanta e jerih coupa. Hayjor i none. Yadetu, o jerih coupa.

Su cali a hayjor i ciudon, nim i vardei ca a coupa a ancem hue.

## Tips

a [subject] i [verb] e [direct object] u [indirect object, to, for]  
a...a... = is/are (copula)  
ancem = turn, rotate  
anja = use, tool  
anopu = lose  
ca = that, which (connector)  
cali = when (what+time)  
ciuseri = row, series  
ciudon = show, demonstrate  
copei = search, seek  
coupa = card  
daelipo = predict, prophecy  
dami = exist, there is  
de = of, from  
doum = cannot (an)do + um  
eledora = fate, destiny  
eodani = meet, trust  
eodyel = join, together  
en = location (at, in)  
falen = child  
hayjor = she (feminine)  
hue = resultant state, in a state of having been acted upon  
jerih = tower  
lar = once, at some point in the past  
lidastor = year   
mai = but  
mir = many  
nim = I, me  
none = have, possess  
noi = here, this  
noli = now  
o = focus/emphasis marker  
siyal = detect, notice, find  
sora = purpose, goal  
su = and, also, with  
tor ganter = second (two + ganter ordinal)  
-um = no/not  
vanta = hand, hold  
vardei = see, look  
yadetu = finally  
yunitam = special
