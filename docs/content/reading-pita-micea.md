# Oimel de Pita Micea

**Level:** Advanced  
**Topic:** Classic children's story  
**Source:** Beatrix Potter, *The Tale of Peter Rabbit*

---

## Reading

lar, i dami e alem yalgai micea, su haye couya a — Flopsy, Mopsy, Cotton-tail, su Pita.
haya i elemi su haye farejor en malveni janou, jenpur de nedas yaltana leirih.

"noli, nime tornono," i ilaluan a yamirli micea farejor cali lusari i ansautar, "run i do i anvu en jele dou en lea leirih, mai i anvuum en leayo de Byasye McGregor: a rune farejal i selcio noi. farejor McGregor i mo e hay."

"noli, i apavu, su tium. nim i anoluis."

notor, a yamirli micea farejor i anita e jojae su haye lufu-jecai, i anvu. hay i gelna e yogali malvae su alui moaniha malvae.

a Flopsy, Mopsy, su Cotton-tail, caei a haya a yuba yalgai micea, i anvu en lea jele i copei e yolimel moaniha.

mai a Pita, caei a hay a nedas ti, i apavu jetai leayo de Byasye McGregor, su i jasru jenpur lea bortal!

tam ganter, hay i mo e noniu leayo mo su noniu maeni. notor, hay i mo e noniu leayo mo.

notor, hay a netor toulum, i anvu i copei e noniu leayo mo.

mai en leayo jenparo, caei i hay i siyal mai a Byasye McGregor!

a Byasye McGregor i anvu en haye apavu-apavu jenpur, i anyar e yalgai leayo mo. mai hay i aparih su i apavu jetai Pita, i asufu e haye ja su i ilaluan, "i asfe, titatahai!"

a Pita i nedas tohpu. hay i apavu neloa en leayo, caora hay i siyal-um e anivari-jetai lea bortal.

hay i anopu e haye tam yespai en leayo mo, su haye tor yespai en maede.

notor anoluhar, hay i apavu en alem apavu su neiden yasoi. ilie hay i raisoi neloa daefer hay i anvu-um en moaniha jecai, su hay i anelu hue de yaltan yesrel antori. a yesrel a yoyol su yosari antori, yamirli-um.

a Pita i daelan-um de neiden. hay i asetowa yaltan. mai noniu yuba muhpili i varsus e haye asetowa, i anifi su i copei-ilaluan u hay i anvu.

a Byasye McGregor i anifi su haye ja, caei hay i anidai i bontame en Pita jetai ganrih. mai a Pita i jasru anolu ilteli, hay i anopu e haye yesrel.

su i apavu en bo-janou, i aparih en molbo. a molbo i do i hehou noi, daefer hay a nedas moje en hay.

a Byasye McGregor i daelan ca a Pita a hehou-dami en bo-janou, ilie jenpur leayo luyar. hay i ansautar i ancem e haya ilteli ilteli, i vardei jenpur haye.

notor, a Pita i asucin — "asucin!" a Byasye McGregor i apavu jetai hay noli!

hay i anidai i bontame e haye apavu en Pita jetai ganrih. a Pita i aparih anolu en borlu, i anopu-anvu e par leayo mo. a borlu i yalgai cane Byasye McGregor i do i loi jenpur, su hay i toului-asfe i apavu jetai Pita. hay i anivari en haye anye.

a Pita i apafene i aslufu. hay a tohpu su hay i siyal-um e jetai. hay i none e nedas moje de apafene en noi molbo.

notor lirul, hay i ansautar i anvu lili lili — yasoi-um nedas, i vardei neloa.

hay i siyal e bortal en borcai. mai a bortal a javer hue, su i borlu-um u yalgai micea i jasru jenpur.

a yamirli mujas i apavu anolu su anivari en leirih bortal, hay i be e maeni u haye fare en leirih. a Pita i copei-ilaluan u hay e jetai lea bortal, mai a mujas i none e yaltan maeni en haye mo, su hay i doum i ilaluan. hay i ancem haye varodu jetai Pita. a Pita i ansautar i asetowa.

notor, hay i anidai i siyal e jetai neloa en leayo, mai hay a neiden-um nedas. notor, hay i anifi en moje-janou cadom a Byasye McGregor i anye haye moje-molbo. a yoyar miau i vardei e yosari moje-mi lirul. hay i apafene nedas lirul, mai notor notor haye javari i jasru no dami. a Pita i daelan ca i yuba-um i ilaluan u hay. hay i varsular de miau de haye micea fare yalgai Benjamin.

hay i anivari jetai bo-janou. mai tonan, widuo u hay, hay i varsus e haye ja — scr-r-ritch, scratch, scratch. a Pita i apavu jenpur jecai. mai notor, cali i dami-um, hay i anolu, su i apavu ganrih en anvu-jojae, i ilofun-vardei. tam ganter hay i vardei a Byasye McGregor i anye e masloma. hay i ancem jetai ho Pita, su hayeho en lea bortal!

a Pita i anolu en anvu-jojae yasoi-um, i ansautar i apavu yasoi ca hay i do, jenparo noniu yolimel moaniha leirih.

a Byasye McGregor i siyal e hay en jele jeluin, mai a Pita i ti-um. hay i jasru jenpur lea bortal, su hay i elemi yuba yadetu en leirih anolu de leayo.

a Byasye McGregor i apaimai e yalgai yesrel su yespai u muhpili-tohpu-dia, i tohpu-anvu e yolimel muhpili.

a Pita i apavu-um su vardei-um jetai haye ho, notam hay i anifi en haye bo en yaltana leirih.

hay a nedas toulum cali hay i asfene en yuba malveni de micea janou, su i antori-um haye vardei. haye farejor i boemo lirul. hay i copei ca hay i anyar de haye yesrel su yespai. a tor ganter yalgai yesrel su yespai ca Pita i anopu en tor lipardas!

bi tohpuis nim i ilaluan ca a Pita i yuba-um nedas en noli li.

haye farejor i bontame e hay en bonfene, su i anye e leirih moje. hay i anona e tam mahon u Pita!

"tam mahon, i mo cali i asfene."

mai a Flopsy, Mopsy, su Cotton-tail i none e malvae su moulu su yolimel moaniha u haye li mo.

<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Once upon a time there were four little Rabbits, and their names were — Flopsy, Mopsy, Cotton-tail, and Peter.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">They lived with their Mother in a sand-bank, underneath the root of a very big fir tree.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">"Now, my dears," said old Mrs. Rabbit one morning, "you may go into the fields or down the lane, but don't go into Mr. McGregor's garden: your Father had an accident there. Mrs. McGregor ate him."</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">"Now, run along, and don't be bad. I am going out."</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Then old Mrs. Rabbit took a basket and her umbrella, and went out. She bought a brown loaf and five berry buns.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cotton-tail, who were good little bunnies, went down the lane to gather blackberries.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">But Peter, who was very naughty, ran toward Mr. McGregor's garden and squeezed under the gate!</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">First he ate some garden vegetables and some beans. Then he ate some more garden vegetables.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Then, a little tired, he went to look for more garden vegetables.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">But at the far end of the garden, who should he find but Mr. McGregor!</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Mr. McGregor was crawling along, planting young vegetables. But he jumped up and ran after Peter, waving his tool and calling out, "Stop, thief!"</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Peter was terribly sad. He ran all over the garden, because he could not find the way back to the gate.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">He lost one shoe among the vegetables, and the other shoe among the potatoes.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Then, on all four legs, he went faster. Maybe he would have escaped entirely if he had not run into a berry fence and got caught by the large buttons on his jacket. The jacket was blue with gold buttons, quite new.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Peter gave up hope and cried big tears. But some friendly birds heard his crying, flew to him, and urged him to keep going.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Mr. McGregor came with his tool, intending to place it on top of Peter. But Peter squeezed out just in time, leaving his jacket behind.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">He ran into the toolshed and jumped into a jar. It would have been a good place to hide, but there was too much water in it.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Mr. McGregor was quite sure Peter was hiding in the toolshed, perhaps under a garden lamp. He began turning them over one by one, looking underneath.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Then Peter sneezed — "Achoo!" Mr. McGregor came after him at once!</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">He tried to put his foot on Peter, who jumped out of a window, knocking over three plants. The window was too small for Mr. McGregor, and he was tired of chasing Peter. He went back to his work.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Peter sat down to catch his breath. He was sad and couldn't find his way. He still had too much water on him from sitting in that jar.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">After a while, he began to move again, slowly, slowly — not very fast, looking all around.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">He found a door in a wall. But the door was locked, and there was no room for a little rabbit to squeeze underneath.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">An old mouse was running in and out by the tree door, carrying beans to her family in the wood. Peter asked her the way to the gate, but the mouse had such a large bean in her mouth that she couldn't answer. She only shook her head at him. Peter began to cry.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Then he tried to find his way straight across the garden, but he was very lost. He came to a pond where Mr. McGregor filled his water jars. A white cat was staring at gold fish in the water. She sat very still, but now and then her tail would twitch as if alive. Peter thought it best not to speak to her. He had heard about cats from his young rabbit cousin, little Benjamin.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">He went back toward the toolshed. But suddenly, close to him, he heard a tool — scr-r-ritch, scratch, scratch. Peter ran under the fence. But after a while, when nothing happened, he came out and climbed up on a wheelbarrow and peeked over. The first thing he saw was Mr. McGregor working with onions. He was turned away from Peter — and beyond him was the gate!</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Peter got down from the wheelbarrow quietly, and started running as fast as he could, behind some black-currant bushes.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Mr. McGregor spotted him at the corner, but Peter didn't care. He slipped under the gate, and was finally safe in the wood outside the garden.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Mr. McGregor hung up the little jacket and shoes for a scarecrow, to frighten the blackbirds.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">Peter never stopped running or looked behind him until he arrived home at the big fir tree.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">He was so tired that he fell asleep on the nice soft sand of the rabbit-hole, and closed his eyes. His mother was cooking. She wondered what he had done with his clothes. It was the second little jacket and pair of shoes Peter had lost in two months!</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">I am sorry to say that Peter was not well that evening.</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">His mother put him to bed and made herbal tea. She gave one spoonful to Peter!</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem 0;">"One spoonful, to be taken at bedtime."</p>
<p style="margin: 0;">But Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cotton-tail had bread and milk and blackberries for supper.</p>
</div>
