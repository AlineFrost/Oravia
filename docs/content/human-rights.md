# Heoloa Eoco de Ilheis Rainevi  

**Level:** Advanced  
**Topic:** Universal Declaration of Human Rights 

---  

## Reading  

### Notams Roudi  

Caora a ilivargor de bofis gaoleo su gaomem ilheis rainevi ca sunya ilhei i do i anita de haya, de neloa eoleo de ilheis famei, a jeodeh de eloele, dael su elonor en heodom;  

Caora a eorenum su ho eoren de ilheis rainevi i dosaba e titi elori ca i tohnaru e ilhei iliroyar, su a heodom cadom ilhei i oila e eloele u ilahai su ilisu, su eloele de tohvor su anifou, i eoco hue no anodu iloi de ganyu ilhei;  

Caora a raiver de ilheis rainevi anja raihucu a anifou, suum ilhei i vil i anita, no yadetu jenon, e raidoi tidoi tiraitar su tiuri;  

Caora a neiden de eofa dameo jenrein radom a anifou;  

Caora ilhei de Radoms Eodyel i eocoar lili en haya coupa e elasu u jeodeh ilheis rainevi, u bofis gaoleo su gaoleo de ilhei, su u gaomem rainevi de faejal su faejor, su i anroar i neiden e rafa neiden su yuba ganyu de elemi en neron eloele;  

Caora eoleo radom i elodaoar i anleo su Radoms Eodyel sora i gaet e heoloa eoren su anja de ilheis rainevi su jeodeh eloele;  

Caora a ganyu ilinou de noi rainevi su eloele a anodu anifou sora yaldih gaet de noi elodao;  

Eta, Geyelu de Radoms Eodyel i eoco e noi Heoloa Eoco de Ilheis Rainevi no ganyu gavol de elepan sora neloa ilhei su neloa radom.  

Nehen ilhei su nehen eoras de rafa i anelem nolili e noi eoco en iliroyar, i tane i anse anja roena su roesau sora i neiden e eoren u noi rainevi su eloele. Anja neiden elori en radom su jenrein radom, i tane i raiver e heoloa su gaet ilivargor su anja de noi rainevi, en ilhei de eoleo radom su en ilhei de ledom ca haya i raitar.  

### Gon 1  

En fadevi, a neloa ilhei a eloele su gaomem en bofis gaoleo su rainevi.  

Haya i none e yunro su iliroyar, su i tane i elori u rein no fasu.  

### Gon 2  

Neloa ilhei i none e neloa rainevi su eloele ca noi eoco i daca, suum gavios elori.  

Noi i gavioum caora raleo, yoyo, oivamin, varluan, elaora, rastai dou neliu iliro, radom dade dou rafa dade, none, fadevi dou neliu ganter.  

Su, gavios elori i doum i anye caora rastai ganter, raihucu ganter dou jenrein radom ganter de radom dou ledom ca ilhei i eoleo en hay, ia dou a hay a elihei raitar, a raidana, i raitar hue de neliu, dou i none e neliu linu en raitar.  

### Gon 3  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u elemi, eloele su anever de elihei.  

### Gon 4  

Sunya ilhei i vanta hue no ticati dou i pohnen hue ca i anse no ticati.  

A ticati su ticatis gehvio a tolenu en neloa dairan.  

### Gon 5  

Sunya ilhei i pohnens tonul hue, dou i tiuri hue non titi dou elileoum, dou anja elori ca i ganou e bofis gaoleo, dou i dohti hue no noi.  

### Gon 6  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi ca raihucu i ilivargor e hay no ilhei en neloa dom.  

### Gon 7  

Neloa ilhei a gaomem en raihucu su i none e gaomem raiver de raihucu suum gavios elori.  

Neloa i none su e gaomem raiver tidoi gavios elori ca i tiuri e noi eoco, su tidoi anse ca i dosaba e gavios elori.  

### Gon 8  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u gaet raihucus selota de radom raihucus anros eoras ca i ando i anro, tidoi elori ca i tiuri e jeodeh rainevi ca radoms coupa dou raihucu i anona u hay.  

### Gon 9  

Sunya ilhei i rainens sita hue, raivoa hue dou dohtei hue non simia.  

### Gon 10  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi, non yaldih gaomem, u gaolan su rafa raihucus roejo de raihucus anros eoras, cali i anro e hay rainevi su dohvil, dou noniu tivonpu de tiuan tidoi hay. Noi eoras a gaohei, su neliu i dohtarum e hay.  

### Gon 11  

1. Neloa ilhei ca i tivonpu hue de dohti tiuan i none e rainevi ca i iliro hue no tiliraum linu raihucu i ciudon e hay tilira, en rafa raihucus roejo cadom hay i none e neloa anifou raiver sora elihei.  

2. Sunya ilhei i tilira hue de dohti tiuan caora elori dou elorium ca a dohti tiuanum anja radom raihucu dou jenrein radom raihucu en litam cali noi i anye. A dohti ca a veihoh ga dohti ca i anore hue en noi litam a tolenu.  

### Gon 12  

Simia tiuri en bevas, famei, bo dou couyen de ilhei a tolenu. Tuapa tidoi eoren su yamirhei de ilhei a tolenu.  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u raihucus raiver tidoi noi tiuri su tuapa.  

### Gon 13  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u eloele anvu su anelem en bofi linu de nehen radom.  

2. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi i anocari de noniu radom, noder de hay radom, su i anivari u hay radom.  

### Gon 14  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi i copei su i oila e railem en neliu radom de anlaro tiuri.  

2. Noi rainevi i doum i anja cali ilhei i copei hue anja raihucu caora elireva tiuan ca a rastaium, dou caora elori tidoi dasora su rodel de Radoms Eodyel.  

### Gon 15  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u radoms eoleo.  

2. Sunya ilhei i anopu e hay radoms eoleo non simia, dou i toleu e hay rainevi i davio e radoms eoleo.  

### Gon 16  

1. Faejal su faejor ca a yamirli norfih, suum noniu linu caora raleo, radoms eoleo dou elaora, i none e rainevi i faigel su i ansau e famei. Haya i none e gaomem rainevi jetai faigel, lilon faigel, su cali faigel i yadetu.  

2. Faigel i tane i ansau dantam anja eloele su yaldih dasu de sutor faiboris.  

3. A famei a dade su jeodeh eodyel de rafa, su i none e rainevi u raiver de rafa su raitar.  

### Gon 17  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u none, elihei dou su neliu.  

2. Sunya ilhei i anopu e hay none non simia.  

### Gon 18  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u eloele de iliro, iliroyar su elaora.  

Noi rainevi i none su e eloele i davio e hay elaora dou ilisu, su i ciudon e hay elaora dou ilisu elihei dou su neliu, en rafa dou en bevas, anja roena, elori, elano su elarul.  

### Gon 19  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u eloele de iliro su ilavos.  

Noi rainevi i none su e eloele i anelem e iliro suum tiuri, su i copei, i cohua, su i cohela e coman su iliora anja noniu ranco, noder jenrein radom.  

### Gon 20  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u eloele elonor geyelu su eoras.  

2. I tolenu ca noniu ilhei i pohnen e neliu ca i eoleo en eoras.  

### Gon 21  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi i anleo en raitar de hay radom, jeciuta dou anja dano ca i anipas hue non eloele.  

2. Neloa ilhei i none e gaomem rainevi i anse en rafa anliulor de hay radom.  

3. A anidai de ilhei a jeodeh de pohnen de raitar. Noi anidai i tane i ciudon en lirul su elireva rapas cadom neloa ilhei i none e gaomem rainevi i anipas, anja hevas anipas dou neliu gaomem eloele semvir.  

### Gon 22  

Neloa ilhei, no eoleo de rafa, i none e rainevi u rafas anever.  

I none su e rainevi ca i anifou u bofis gaoleo su eloele neiden de elihei: rage rainevi, rafa rainevi su rasora rainevi.  

Noi i tane i gaet anja anse de hay radom su anleo jenrein radom, cane eoras su rani de nehen radom i ando.  

### Gon 23  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u anse, u eloele anipas de anse, u dael su yuba veinal de anse, su u raiver de anseum.  

2. Neloa ilhei, suum gavios elori, i none e rainevi u gaomem geta sora gaomem anse.  

3. Neloa anse hai i none e rainevi u dael su yuba geta ca i anever e norfih elemi sora hay su famei, elemi ca a elonevi de bofis gaoleo. Daehun noi i norfihum, i tane i elomiu anja neliu rafa raiver.  

4. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi i anye su i eodyel e anses eoras sora raiver e hay oihi.  

### Gon 24  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u anefene su oi li, su u gaolan linu de anse litetan su lirul anefene litam anja geta.  

### Gon 25  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u ganyu elemi ca a norfih sora vei su yuba elemi de hay su famei. Noi i none e mo, yes, bo, velan su anifou rafa anliulor.  

I none su e rainevi u anever cali i anseum, i touvei, a ganou ando veinal, faibor i tidetu, a hay a yamirli, dou i anopu e rani sora elemi caora veinal ca hay i doum i dohtar.  

2. Farejor su falen i none e rainevi u yunitam eover su elomiu. Neloa falen i none e gaomem rafa raiver, noder faigel ganter de haya fare.  

### Gon 26  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u roena. Jeodeh roena i tane a suum gerina, su i vil i anita hue de neloa falen. Ranvir su anse roena i tane i dami en mir dom. Ganrih roena i tane a gaomem do anita hue u neloa ilhei anja elonevi.  

2. Roena i tane i antai jetai yaldih neiden de elihei su jetai neiden de eoren u ilheis rainevi su jeodeh eloele. I tane i neiden e ilinou, dopim su eofa jenrein neloa radom, raleo eodyel su elaora eodyel, su i anleo u elori de Radoms Eodyel sora i anelem e elonor.  

3. Fare i none e notam rainevi i anipas e semiru de roena ca haya falen i anita.  

### Gon 27  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi i eloele anleo en rasora elemi de rafa, i oila e ciumar, su i anleo en rovir neiden su i anita e hay anlor.  

2. Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u raiver de elirota su dama oihi ca i dade de hay anye hue en rovir, roudi dou ciumar.  

### Gon 28  

Neloa ilhei i none e rainevi u rafa semtela su jenrein radom semtela cadom noi rainevi su eloele i do i gaet hue yaldih.  

### Gon 29  

1. Neloa ilhei i none e dohvil u rafa, ca dantam en hay ilhei i do i neiden e elihei non eloele su yaldih.  

2. En anja de hay rainevi su eloele, ilhei i do i none dantam linu ca raihucu i anefe, sora i anever e ilivargor su eoren de rainevi su eloele de neliu, su sora i gaet e dael anifou de elirota, rafa semtela su ganyu yuba elemi en rafa cadom ilhei i anipas e raitar.  

3. Noi rainevi su eloele i doum i anja en non ca a tidoi dasora su rodel de Radoms Eodyel.  

### Gon 30  

Sunya ilwol en noi eoco i doum i iliro hue canon ca i anona u noniu radom, eodyel dou ilhei e rainevi i anse dou i elori sora i siur e noniu rainevi su eloele ca noi i daca.

<div id="translation-human-rights" style="display: none; background: #c8e6c9; padding: 1.5rem; border-left: 4px solid #43a047; border-radius: 4px; margin: 2rem 0;">  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Preamble.</strong> Recognition of every person's inherent worth and equal rights that cannot be taken away is the basis of freedom, justice and peace. Disregard for human rights has led to cruel acts that outrage human conscience; people therefore hope for a world with freedom of speech and belief and freedom from fear and need. Human rights need legal protection so that people are not driven, as a last resort, to rebellion against tyranny and abuse. Friendly relations among nations should develop. The peoples of the United Nations reaffirm their faith in fundamental human rights, human worth, equal rights of men and women, social progress, better living standards and greater freedom. Member nations pledge to cooperate with the United Nations to achieve universal respect for and observance of these rights and freedoms. A common understanding of them is essential. The United Nations Assembly therefore proclaims this Declaration as a common standard for all people and nations, to be kept in mind, taught, promoted, and progressively secured both nationally and internationally.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 1.</strong> Everyone is born free and equal in inherent worth and rights. People have reason and conscience and should treat one another like siblings.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 2.</strong> Everyone has every right and freedom in this Declaration without discrimination based on race or ethnicity, color, sex, language, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, property, birth, or other status. The political, legal, or international status of a person's country or territory also cannot justify discrimination, whether that territory governs itself, is a trust or dependency, is governed by another power, or has another limitation on self-rule.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 3.</strong> Everyone has the right to life, liberty and personal security.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 4.</strong> No one may be held as a slave or forced into servitude. Slavery and the slave trade are forbidden in every form.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 5.</strong> No one may be tortured or subjected to cruel, inhumane or degrading treatment or punishment.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 6.</strong> Everyone has the right to be recognized everywhere as a person under the law.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 7.</strong> Everyone is equal before the law and has equal legal protection without discrimination, including protection from discrimination that violates this Declaration and from acts that encourage such discrimination.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 8.</strong> Everyone has the right to an effective legal remedy from competent national courts when fundamental rights granted by law or a constitution are violated.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 9.</strong> No one may be arbitrarily arrested, imprisoned or exiled.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 10.</strong> Everyone is equally entitled to a fair and public hearing by an independent and impartial tribunal when their rights, obligations, or a criminal charge against them are decided.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 11.</strong> A person accused of a crime has the right to be treated as not guilty until guilt is proved according to law in a public trial with the protections needed for a defense. No one may be convicted for an act or omission that was not a crime under national or international law when it occurred, and no harsher punishment may be imposed than the one then allowed.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 12.</strong> No one may face arbitrary interference with privacy, family, home or correspondence, or attacks on honor and reputation. Everyone has the right to legal protection from such interference and attacks.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 13.</strong> Everyone has freedom to move and live within a country's boundaries, and may leave any country, including their own, and return to their own country.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 14.</strong> Everyone has the right to seek and enjoy asylum in another country from persecution. This does not apply to genuine prosecution for non-political crimes or for acts contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 15.</strong> Everyone has the right to a nationality. No one may arbitrarily lose it or be denied the right to change it.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 16.</strong> Adult men and women may marry and form a family without restrictions based on race, nationality or religion, and have equal rights before, during and at the end of marriage. Marriage requires the free and full consent of both intending spouses. The family is a fundamental social unit and has a right to protection by society and government.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 17.</strong> Everyone has the right to own property alone or together with others. No one may arbitrarily lose their property.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 18.</strong> Everyone has freedom of thought, conscience and religion, including freedom to change religion or belief and to express it alone or with others, publicly or privately, through teaching, practice, worship and observance.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 19.</strong> Everyone has freedom of opinion and expression, including the freedom to hold opinions without interference and to seek, receive and share information and ideas through any medium across national boundaries.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 20.</strong> Everyone has the right to peaceful assembly and association. No one may be forced to belong to an association.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 21.</strong> Everyone may participate in the government of their country directly or through freely chosen representatives and has equal access to public service. Government authority must rest on the people's will, expressed through regular genuine elections with universal and equal participation and secret or equivalently free voting.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 22.</strong> Everyone, as a member of society, has a right to social security and to the economic, social and cultural rights needed for inherent worth and free self-development, achieved through national effort and international cooperation according to each country's organization and resources.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 23.</strong> Everyone has a right to work, freely choose employment, have just and favorable work conditions, and receive protection from unemployment. Equal work deserves equal pay without discrimination. Workers have a right to fair pay sufficient for a life worthy of human dignity for themselves and their families, supplemented when necessary by social protection. Everyone may form and join worker organizations to protect their interests.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 24.</strong> Everyone has a right to rest and leisure, including reasonable limits on working hours and recurring paid holidays.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 25.</strong> Everyone has a right to a standard of living sufficient for the health and well-being of themselves and their family, including food, clothing, housing, medical care and necessary social services. People also have a right to security when livelihood is threatened by unemployment, illness, disability, a spouse's death, old age, or circumstances beyond their control. Mothers and children deserve special care and assistance, and all children deserve equal social protection regardless of their parents' marital status.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 26.</strong> Everyone has a right to education. Fundamental education should be free and compulsory; technical and vocational education should be broadly available; higher education should be equally accessible based on merit. Education should fully develop the person, strengthen respect for human rights and freedoms, promote understanding, tolerance and friendship among nations and racial and religious groups, and support the United Nations' work for peace. Parents have the first right to choose the kind of education their children receive.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 27.</strong> Everyone may freely participate in the cultural life of the community, enjoy art, and share in scientific progress and its benefits. Creators also have a right to protection of the moral and material interests arising from their scientific, written or artistic creations.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 28.</strong> Everyone has a right to a social and international order in which the rights and freedoms in this Declaration can be fully realized.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0 0 0.75rem 0;"><strong>Article 29.</strong> Everyone has duties to the community, because free and full self-development is possible only within community. Rights and freedoms may be limited only by law, and only as needed to respect the rights and freedoms of others and meet the just needs of morality, public order and general welfare in a society where people choose their government. These rights and freedoms may never be used against the purposes and principles of the United Nations.</p>  
<p style="margin: 0;"><strong>Article 30.</strong> Nothing in this Declaration gives any country, group or person a right to act with the aim of destroying any of the rights and freedoms stated here.</p>
