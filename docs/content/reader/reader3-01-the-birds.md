# 3.1 — The Birds

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/3.1.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>En liyar, mir yoyo muhpili i anvu en luyol no eodyel, su i yavuson galiu e jetai.</p>
<p>Cali lufu i davio no pohem, tam muhpili i aniyel ganou su i anelem en leirih, lilon a neliu i helufu ganrih.</p>
<p>De leirih, hay i vardei e mu ca i apaleno en lenous lea.</p>
<p>Notor a lufu i gafei, su o muhpili i lili garel.</p>
<p>Hay i anvu ganrih, i eodyel e neliu, su a eodyel i ancem jetai lusari.</p>
<p>Lisoi hayas i davio no yalgai en nordau luyol su i vardeium hue.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>In the morning, many colorful birds move through the sky as a group, changing direction little by little.</p>
<p>When the wind grows strong, one bird comes lower and settles in a tree while the others continue above.</p>
<p>From the tree, it watches small animals that dig in the prairie.</p>
<p>Then the wind weakens, and the bird rises again.</p>
<p>It moves upward, rejoins the others, and the group turns in the direction of the sun.</p>
<p>Soon they become small in the distant sky and are no longer seen.</p>

</details>

## Try to answer in Oravia

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p><strong>Tam muhpili i anelem en leirih ceora?</strong></p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Check answer</summary>

<p><strong>English question:</strong> Why does one bird settle in the tree?</p>

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p><strong>Possible answer:</strong> Caora a lufu i davio no pohem.</p>
</div>

<p><strong>English:</strong> Because the wind becomes strong.</p>

</details>
