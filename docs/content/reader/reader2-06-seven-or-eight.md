# 2.6 — Seven or Eight?

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/2.6.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>Nim i iliro ca a eomsu en peri litetan.</p>
<p>Nim faibor i ilaluan ca a eomsu en auta litetan.</p>
<p>Eta nimas i ilianum cali anvu.</p>
<p>I corior e eofa su i ilace:</p>
<p>"Eon! Run eomsu, en litam peri dou auta?"</p>
<p>A eofa i asunes su i ilaluan: "Tenda!"</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>I think the party is at seven o'clock.</p>
<p>My partner thinks that the party is at eight o'clock.</p>
<p>So we don't know when to go.</p>
<p>I call my friend and ask:</p>
<p>"Hello! Your party, is it at seven or eight today?”</p>
<p>My friend laughs and says: "Nine!"</p>

</details>

**Try to answer in Oravia:** Celi a eomsu de eofa?
