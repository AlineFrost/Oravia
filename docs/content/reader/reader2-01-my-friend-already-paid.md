# 2.1 — Returned Money

[← All graded readers](reader.md)

<!-- IMAGE: Two friends buying food; the woman taking payment immediately gives the money back to one of them. -->

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/2.1.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p>Nim su eofa i mo en bevio.</p>
<p>A nim i anona e gerina u faejor de bevio.</p>
<p>A faejor i anona e gerina u nim.</p>
<p>Lili a nim i anona u faejor, su lili a hay i anona u nim.</p>
<p>Eta nim i ilace: “Ceora?”</p>
<p>A faejor i ilaluan: “A run eofa i gerinar.”</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>My friend and I eat at a shop.</p>
<p>I give money to the woman of the shop.</p>
<p>She gives the money back to me.</p>
<p>Again I give the money to the woman, and again she gives it to me.</p>
<p>So I ask, “Why?”</p>
<p>The woman says, “Your friend has paid.”</p>

</details>
