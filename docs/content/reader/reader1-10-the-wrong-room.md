<p>Nim i eodani e nim faibor.</p>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>I enter the large room.</p>
<p>I ask, “Where is the gathering?”</p>
<p>A woman says, “In the small room.”</p>
<p>I enter the small room and meet my partner.</p>

</details>
