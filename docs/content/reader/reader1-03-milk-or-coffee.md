# 1.3 — Milk or Coffee?

[← All graded readers](reader.md)

<!-- PICTURE TO ADD: Two partners at a table, one with coffee and one with milk. -->

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/1.3.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p>Nim faibor i ilaluan: “I anidai e moulu dou e mogali?”</p>
<p>Nim i ilaluan: “Mogali.”</p>
<p>Hay i anona e mogali u nim.</p>
<p>Hay i mouje e moulu.</p>
<p>Nim i dapas e mogali.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>My partner says, “Do you want milk or coffee?”</p>
<p>I say, “Coffee.”</p>
<p>They give me coffee.</p>
<p>They drink milk.</p>
<p>I like the coffee.</p>

</details>

**Try to answer in Oravia:** What does each person drink?
