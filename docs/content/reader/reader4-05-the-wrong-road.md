# 4.5 — The Wrong Road

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

![Two cyclists at a fork in a road, checking a phone after choosing the wrong direction toward a forest.](../../images/graded-readers/reader4/05-the-wrong-road.jpg)

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>En liyar, nim su eofa i anocari de wiltan en beitor, i anidai i anifi en lealei notar limel.</p>
<p>Cali jenon i gavio en tor jetai, eofa i anipas e jenmue jenon su i ilaluan ca hay i miotan e hay de yamirli anvu.</p>
<p>Nim i ilofun, mai ca i noneum e yuba neliu ora, i dasu.</p>
<p>En litegai, jenon i davio e yalgai su yudur, su nim i vardei e corior ca i ciudon ca nimas i anvu en ho jetai.</p>
<p>“Nimas i tane i anivari,” nim i ilaluan.</p>
<p>Eofa i dasuum notar, caora i iliro ca jenon i do i ancem lisoi, mai cali i garel en neliu jenon, a lealei i davio e nordau.</p>
<p>Notor hay i vardei e corior su i asular. “Yuba. Nime selcio.”</p>
<p>Nimas i anivari su i anipas e neliu jetai, mai noli i vardei e corior notar neloa gavio de jenon.</p>
<p>Cali nimas i anifi en beilem widuo de lealei, eofa i ilaluan: “A ho jetai i anona e yunitam anvu u nimas.”</p>
<p>Nim i daco: “En neliu litam, nimas i anipas e yunitam jetai notar.”</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>In the morning, my friend and I leave the city by bicycle, hoping to reach a forest before evening.</p>
<p>When the road divides in two, my friend chooses the left road and says they remember it from an earlier trip.</p>
<p>I am uncertain, but because I have no better idea, I agree.</p>
<p>A few minutes later the road becomes narrow and difficult, and I check the phone, which shows that we are traveling in the opposite direction.</p>
<p>“We should go back,” I say.</p>
<p>My friend disagrees at first because they think the road will turn soon, but when we climb another stretch, the forest becomes even farther away.</p>
<p>Then they check the phone too and laugh. “Fine. My mistake.”</p>
<p>We go back and choose the other direction, but now we check the map before every change in the road.</p>
<p>When we reach a station near the forest, my friend says, “The wrong direction gave us an interesting trip.”</p>
<p>I reply, “Next time, let’s choose the interesting direction after the correct one.”</p>

</details>
