# 4.1 — The Old Photograph in the Restaurant

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

![A person and parent eating in a restaurant while an old photograph on the wall shows a young person who looks strikingly familiar.](../../images/graded-readers/reader4/01-the-old-photograph-in-the-restaurant.jpg)

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>En limel, nim su fare i mo en mo dom ca hay lirul i eodenbor cali i dami en wiltan.</p>
<p>Lilon fare i ilahai, nim i vardei e yamirli ciureva en borcai su i anepou i mo: a falte ilhei en hay a nedas no nim.</p>
<p>“Cei a hay?” nim i ilace.</p>
<p>Fare i vardei e ciureva, su en litegai i asular. “A hay a nim.”</p>
<p>Nim i ilace lili: “Run?”</p>
<p>Hay i ilaluan ca a ciureva i anyear noi en yamirli lidastor, cali hay i anse widuo su i mo noi neauta lirul.</p>
<p>Nim i vardei e ciureva, notor e fare, su i siyal e gaomem varodu non ca nim i vardeium notam.</p>
<p>“Noli i ilivargor e run,” nim i ilaluan.</p>
<p>Fare i daco: “Nim i ilivargor e nim su.”</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>One evening, my parent and I eat at an eating place that they often visit when they are in the city.</p>
<p>While my parent is talking, I notice an old photograph on the wall and stop eating: the young person in it looks remarkably like me.</p>
<p>“Who is that?” I ask.</p>
<p>My parent looks at the photograph and, after a moment, smiles. “That’s me.”</p>
<p>I ask again, “You?”</p>
<p>They explain that the photograph was taken there many years ago, when they worked nearby and ate there often.</p>
<p>I look at the photograph, then at my parent, and notice the same expression on their face that I had not seen at first.</p>
<p>“Now I recognize you,” I say.</p>
<p>My parent replies, “I recognize myself too.”</p>

</details>
