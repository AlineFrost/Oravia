# 4.2 — The Storm

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

![Bus passengers sheltering inside a nearby building while heavy rain floods the lower floor and the river rises.](../../images/graded-readers/reader4/02-the-storm.jpg)

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>En liyar, lupupi i apanou yasoi en wiltan, su wimlua i neiden cali jenon widuo i davio e tiuvor.</p>
<p>Raitar i eoco ca ilhei i tane i anonuve e jenon, mai beitan ca norven en hay i anepou en jenparo de bogor.</p>
<p>Roena hai en beitan i ilaluan ca a bogor a anever, eta ilhei i anocari de beitan su i bortal; tam ilhei i anisu e yejae u fare ca i vanta e falni.</p>
<p>Lisoi wa i bortal en jenpur gaone, su hayas i anvu en boelori en ganrih gaone lilon i anelem ca lupupi i gafei.</p>
<p>En auta litetan, raitar i eoco ca a jenon a anever lili.</p>
<p>Cali hayas i anocari de bogor, a lufu norven i gafei, mai jenon i dami e mir wa; eta hayas i anvu vanpai, yasoi su eoren, jetai neliu beilem.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>In the morning, heavy rain falls over the city, and the river rises as the nearby road becomes dangerous.</p>
<p>The government announces that people should avoid the road, but a bus already there stops in front of a building.</p>
<p>A teacher on the bus says the building is safe, so the passengers leave the bus and enter; one person carries a bag for a parent holding a baby.</p>
<p>Soon water enters the lower floor, and everyone moves to a room upstairs while they wait for the rain to weaken.</p>
<p>At eight o’clock, the government announces that the road is safe again.</p>
<p>When they leave the building, the wind has already weakened, but the road still has a lot of water; therefore they continue carefully on foot toward another station.</p>

</details>
