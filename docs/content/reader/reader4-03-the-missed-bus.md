# 4.3 — Lunch Next Week

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

<!-- IMAGE NEEDED: Two friends talking after work, one stressed about a project while they arrange breakfast for the following week. -->

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>Cali Lina i eodani e Ana notar anse, i ilace: “Cenon a run?”</p>
<p>Ana i daco: “Toharou. Nim i ansau e ni ciuan en anse, su neloa noi liperi i nomie no nedastam apasoi.”</p>
<p>Lina i ilaluan: “Run i anidai i mo su nim en litam?”</p>
<p>“Bi tohpu, i doum. Nim i dami en anse en neloa noi liperi.”</p>
<p>“En liperis?”</p>
<p>Ana i iliro en litegai. “Ia. Celi?”</p>
<p>“En dastor litetan? Nimas i do i mo su i ilahai.”</p>
<p>“Nim i do su en liyar. Ce en auta litetan? Nimas i mo notam anse.”</p>
<p>Lina i dasu: “Auta litetan. Su run i do i ilahai u nim canon a eodenbor de run gel fare.”</p>
<p>Ana i asular. “Daehun nimas i dami e yalen liyar, nim i ilahai e neloa.”</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>When Lina meets Ana after work, she asks, “How are you?”</p>
<p>Ana replies, “Stressed. I’m starting a new project at work, and this whole week looks extremely busy.”</p>
<p>Lina says, “Would you like to eat with me today?”</p>
<p>“Unfortunately, I can’t. I’m at work all this week.”</p>
<p>“Next week?”</p>
<p>Ana thinks for a moment. “Yes. When?”</p>
<p>“At twelve? We can eat and talk.”</p>
<p>“I can also do the morning. How about eight? We can eat before work.”</p>
<p>Lina agrees: “Eight o’clock. And you can tell me how the visit from your in-laws went.”</p>
<p>Ana smiles. “If we have a long morning, I’ll tell you everything.”</p>

</details>
