# 2.2 — Meeting Someone

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/2.2.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>En liyar, Kai i eodani e Nara en leayo.</p>
<p>Kai i ilaluan: “Yuba liyar! A nim couya a Kai. Ce a run couya?”</p>
<p>Nara i daco: “A nim couya a Nara. Eon, Kai.”</p>
<p>Kai i ilace: “A run a ce lidastor?”</p>
<p>Nara i daco: “A nim a autadas lidastor. Su run?”</p>
<p>“A nim a das tor. A run ansear e ce?”</p>
<p>“Nim i ansear en gedom. Su run?”</p>
<p>“A nim a roesau dia.”</p>
<p>“I dami a fasu u run?”</p>
<p>“Ia, i dami a tor fasu u nim.”</p>
<p>“De cedom runas?”</p>
<p>“De noi.”</p>
<p>Hayas i mo su i ilahai noi liyar.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>In the morning, Kai meets Nara at a park.</p>
<p>Kai says, “Good morning! My name is Kai. What is your name?”</p>
<p>Nara replies, “My name is Nara. Hello, Kai.”</p>
<p>Kai asks, “How old are you?”</p>
<p>Nara replies, “I am eighty years old. And you?”</p>
<p>“I am twelve years old. What was your work?”</p>
<p>“I am a teacher. And you?”</p>
<p>“I am a student.”</p>
<p>“Do you have siblings?”</p>
<p>“Yes, I have two siblings.”</p>
<p>“Where are you all from?.”</p>
<p>“From here.”</p>
<p>They eat and talk that morning.</p>

</details>
