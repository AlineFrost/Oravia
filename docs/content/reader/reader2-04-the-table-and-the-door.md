# 2.4 — The Table and the Door

[← All graded readers](reader.md)

<!-- IMAGE: A large table stopped at a very small doorway while a parent and child look at the problem. -->

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/2.4.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p>A fare i gehna e yaltan bontame.</p>
<p>Yuba bontame.</p>
<p>Eta hay i anona u fano.</p>
<p>A fano i dapas e bontame.</p>
<p>A bontame a yaltan, mai a bortal de boelori a yalgai.</p>
<p>A fano i bortal en boelori.</p>
<p>A bontame i bortalum.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>A parent buys a large table.</p>
<p>A nice table.</p>
<p>So they give it to their child.</p>
<p>The child likes the table.</p>
<p>The table is large, but the room’s door is small.</p>
<p>The child enters the room.</p>
<p>The table does not.</p>

</details>

---
## Try to answer in Oravia

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p><strong>Ceora a fare i anona e bontame u fano?</strong></p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Check answer</summary>

<p><strong>English question:</strong> Why did the parent give the table to the child?</p>

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p><strong>Possible answer:</strong> Caora a bontame a yuba.</p>
</div>

<p><strong>English:</strong> Because the table is good.</p>

</details>
