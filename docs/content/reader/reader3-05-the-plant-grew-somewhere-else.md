# 3.5 — The Plant Grew Somewhere Else

[← All graded readers](reader.md)

<!-- IMAGE: Two children looking for flowers near one tree, then discovering them beside a different tree after heavy rain. -->

![Two children looking for flowers near one tree, then discovering them beside a different tree after heavy rain.](../../images/graded-readers/reader3/05-the-plant-grew-somewhere-else.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p>En litam, a falen su fasu i anvu en leayo.</p>
<p>Hayas i apaleno e leini en tam leirih.</p>
<p>En limel, a lupupi i apanou yasoi.</p>
<p>En neliu litam, hayas i vardei e tam leirih, mai a leipili i damium.</p>
<p>En par litam, a falen i vardei e leipili en neliu leirih.</p>
<p>Hay i ilace u fare: “Ceora?”</p>
<p>A fare i ilaluan ca a lupupi i anvu e leini.</p>
<p>A fasu i asular.</p>
<p>A falen i ilaluan: “A leini i anidai e neliu leirih.”</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>One day, a child and a sibling go into the garden.</p>
<p>They bury seeds beside one tree.</p>
<p>At night, heavy rain falls.</p>
<p>The next day they look by that tree, but there are no flowers.</p>
<p>Three days later, the child sees flowers beside a different tree.</p>
<p>They ask the parent, “Why?”</p>
<p>The parent says the rain moved the seeds.</p>
<p>The sibling laughs.</p>
<p>The child says, “The seeds wanted a different tree.”</p>

</details>
