# 3.3 — Too Much Salt

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/3.3.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>Fare i lirul boemo e maemiri, mai litam a yalgai fano i neauta anidai i elomiu.</p>
<p>Eta a falen i neron e noniu masha en maemiri.</p>
<p>Notor, hayas i vil i iloi lilon a maemiri i pohlufu.</p>
<p>Caora noi, a falen i ilonya ca i neronar e masha. Eta i neron lili.</p>
<p>A maemiri a anparu. A fa i bontame su i apafene sora mo. A jor fare i anilie i mo, mai i anepou notam tam vardur.  
<p>Jal fare su i anilie, su i ilaluan: “A maemiri a... yunitam!”</p>
<p>Falen i oila-ilace: “Yuba?”</p>
<p>“Yunitam,” fare i daco, su i asela.</p>
<p>Notor falen i sero ca hay i neronar e masha tor li! Mai a hay a yungi caora elomiu, eta i mo e neloa maemiri.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>The parent habitually cooks rice, but today the small child eagerly wants to help.</p>
<p>So the child adds some salt to the rice.</p>
<p>Then, they must wait while the rice boils.</p>
<p>Because of that, the child forgets that they added salt. So they add it again.</p>
<p>The rice is ready. The family puts on the table and sits to eat. The mother tries to eat it, but stops after one bite.</p>
<p>The father too tries, and says, “The rice is… special!”</p>
<p>The child asks happily: “Good?”</p>
<p>“Special,” the parent replies, and smiles.</p>
<p>Then the child remembers that they added salt twice! But they are proud for their help, so they eat all the rice. </p>

</details>
