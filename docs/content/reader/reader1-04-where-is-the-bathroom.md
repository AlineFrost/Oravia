# 1.4 — Where Is the Bathroom?

[← All graded readers](reader.md)

<!-- PICTURE TO ADD: A woman pointing toward a small room where the bathroom is located. -->

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/1.4.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p>Nim i ilaluan u faejor: “Cedom a bosvi?”</p>
<p>Faejor i ilaluan: “En yalgai boelori.”</p>
<p>A nim en yalgai boelori.</p>
<p>A bosvi en boelori.</p>
<p>Nim i dapas e boelori.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>I say to a woman, “Where is the bathroom?”</p>
<p>She says, “In the small room.”</p>
<p>I am in the small room.</p>
<p>The bathroom is in the room.</p>
<p>I like the room.</p>

</details>

**Try to answer in Oravia:** Is the bathroom in a small room or a large room?
