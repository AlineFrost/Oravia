# 1.7 — Seven or Eight?

[← All graded readers](reader.md)

<!-- PICTURE TO ADD: Two people checking whether a gathering begins at seven or eight. -->

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p>Nim i iliro ca a eomsu en litetan peri.</p>
<p>Nim faibor i ilaluan ca a eomsu en litetan auta.</p>
<p>Nimas i ilace u nimas eofa: “Celi a eomsu, peri dou auta?”</p>
<p>Hay i ilaluan: “Teva.”</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>I think the gathering is at seven.</p>
<p>My partner says it is at eight.</p>
<p>I ask, “Seven or eight?”</p>
<p>They say, “Six.”</p>


</details>

**Try to answer in Oravia:** What time do they meet?
