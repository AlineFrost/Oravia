# 2.3 — On the Plane

[← All graded readers](reader.md)

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/2.3.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p>"A run i anidai ce mouje? Moaria mouje dou mogali?"</p>
<p>"Mogali, eori."</p>
<p>"Moval mogali?"</p>
<p>"Um, mai su moulu, eori."</p>
<p>"Run i anidai e mocen su?”</p>
<p>"Ia, anidai. Nim fano en bosvi, eta a nim i do i anita e tor?"</p>
<p>"Nim i do i anona e tor mocen, u run su u hay."</p>
<p>"Eloan!"</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>"Which drink would you like? Apple juice or coffee?"</p>
<p>"Coffee, please."</p>
<p>"Cold coffee?"</p>
<p>"No, but with milk, please."</p>
<p>"Would you like chocolate too?”</p>
<p>"Yes, I do. My kid is in the bathroom, so can I take two?"</p>
<p>"I can give you two chocolates, for you and for them."</p>
<p>"Thank you!"</p>

</details>

## Try to answer in Oravia

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p><strong>A faejor i anona e tor mocen ceora?</strong></p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Check answer</summary>

<p><strong>English question:</strong> Why did the woman give two chocolate bars?</p>

<div class="oravia-reading">
<p><strong>Possible answer:</strong> Caora tam mocen u faejal su tam u hay fano.</p>
</div>

<p><strong>English:</strong> Because it's one chocolate for the man and one for his kid.</p>

</details>
