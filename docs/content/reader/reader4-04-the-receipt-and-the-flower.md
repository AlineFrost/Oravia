# 4.4 — Money in the Jacket

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

<!-- IMAGE NEEDED: A person returning to a friend’s door after remembering that money is still inside an old jacket they just gave away. -->

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>En litam, Ana i anisu e yamirli yesrel ca hay i yesum norven, caora i anidai i anona e hay u eofa.</p>
<p>Cali i anocari de bo de eofa, i miotan ca liperiar hay i anefe e gerina en tam yejae de yesrel.</p>
<p>Ana i anepou en jenon. A gerina norven en bo de eofa.</p>
<p>I anivari yasoi, mai cali eofa i bortal, hay norven i vanta e yesrel su i asular.</p>
<p>“Run i miotan e noniu?” hay i ilace.</p>
<p>Ana i ilaluan ca i anefe e gerina en yesrel, mai i ilianum cadom.</p>
<p>Eofa i copei en jensu de yesrel su i siyal e gerina.</p>
<p>Hay i anona e gerina u Ana, notor i yesrel su i ilaluan: “Run i vanta e gerina. Nim i vanta e yesrel.”</p>
<p>Ana i vardei e hay su i daco: “Dasu.”</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>One day, Ana takes an old jacket that she no longer wears because she wants to give it to a friend.</p>
<p>As she leaves the friend’s house, she remembers that the previous week she put some money in one of the jacket’s pockets.</p>
<p>Ana stops in the road. The money is now inside her friend’s house.</p>
<p>She hurries back, but when the friend opens the door, they are already holding the jacket and smiling.</p>
<p>“Did you remember something?” they ask.</p>
<p>Ana says that she put money in the jacket but does not know where.</p>
<p>The friend searches the parts of the jacket and finds the money.</p>
<p>They give the money to Ana, then put the jacket on and say, “You keep the money. I’m keeping the jacket.”</p>
<p>Ana looks at them and replies, “Agreed.”</p>

</details>
