# 2.5 — One Empty Seat

[← All graded readers](reader.md){ .graded-reader-back }

![Story illustration](../../images/graded-readers/2.5.jpg){ width="480" style="display:block; margin:1.25rem auto 2rem auto;" }

<div class="oravia-reading graded-reader-text">
<p>Nim su eofa i anvu en beitan.</p>
<p>A mir ilhei i dami, eta a tam apafene a jenya.</p>
<p>Eofa i apafene en hay.</p>
<p>En neliu beilem, a yamirli faejor i bortal en beitan.</p>
<p>Eofa i vardei e hay, i ho bonfene, su i ilaluan: “U run.”</p>
<p>Faejor i apafene su i asela.</p>
<p>Nim i vardei e eofa.</p>
<p>“Yuba,” nim i ilaluan.</p>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Show English translation</summary>

<p>My friend and I are on a bus.</p>
<p>There are many people, so one seat is empty.</p>
<p>My friend sits in it.</p>
<p>At the next stop, an older woman gets on the bus.</p>
<p>My friend sees her, stands up, and says, “For you.”</p>
<p>The woman sits down and smiles.</p>
<p>I look at my friend.</p>
<p>“Nice,” I say.</p>

</details>

**Try to answer in Oravia:** Ceora a eofa i ho bonfene?
