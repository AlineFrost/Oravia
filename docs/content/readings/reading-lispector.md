# Clarice Lispector Excerpt

**Level:** Advanced     
**Topic:** A famous excerpt from the expressive prose of Clarice Lispector  

---


## Reading  

Neloa en heodom i ansau de "ia". Pogai i ilaluan u neliu pogai eta a elemi i favi. Mai notam notam-rasuir i dami notam-rasuir de notam-rasuir su i dami sunya lirul su i dami "ia". Nolili i dami. Nim i ilianum ca i dami, mai i ilian ca a heoloa ansaum. Bi iloi sunya i tohbian, nim i do e yugai netam anja mir anse. Lilon i dami ilace en nim, su i damium daco, i anlaro i roudi. Canon i do i ansau en ansau, daehun i ilwol i dateli notam i dateli? Daehun noi oimel i damium, noli bi el. A i iliro a elori. A i tohvi a elireva. Tor eodyel: o nim i roudi ilwol ca nim i roudi.

from A Hora da Estrela

---

## Tips

a...a... = is/are (copula)  
a [subject] i [verb] e [direct object]  
-um = negation  
bi el = hereby (performative — the utterance enacts what it says)  

anlaro = continue, still  
ansau = start  
daco = answer    
dami = exist, there is  
dateli = situation, to happen   
de = from, of  
elemi = life  
elireva = true, accurate  
elori = act, active  
eodyel = together, as one  
favi = birth  
heodom = the world    
heoloa = universe    
ilace = question, ask  
ilian = know  
iliro = think  
ilwol = thing  
oimel = story  
netam = only  
neloa = everything, everyone  
nolili = forever  
notam = first  
pogai = atom  
rasuir = history   
roudi = write  
sunya = 0/10  
tohbian = confusion  
tohvi = feeling   
tor = two  
yugai = easy
