## Commentary

This song is emotional to me because it reflects on the immense human cost of the Portuguese Empire, which is personal to my family history. I interpret the second stanza to be about the people that were enslaved, and not the Portuguese sailors. Now, this does not seem to be the most common interpretation, but I think it's well-supported by the framing of the song as critical to the empire, the start of the second stanza with a reference to the slave ship, and the fact that the land the person longs for is referenced as Luanda.
