# Lesson 1: Pronouns and Your First Oravia Words

## Grammar

Oravia has three singular personal pronouns:

**nim** — I / me  
**run** — you  
**hay** — he / she / it / singular they  

**hay** is gender-neutral. Gender does not have to be marked unless it matters.

Here's something to remember them: *nim* sounds similar to *me*, *run* has *u* in it, and *hay* has *h* like *he* and *she*.

### More than one person? Add -a

Most Oravia words do **not** change for plural. Personal pronouns are the exception. To create the plural personal pronouns, we add -a in the end.

If **run** is *you*, what would you guess for *you all*?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Click to check</summary>

Runa.  
Add **-a**:  

**nim → nima** — I → we    
**run → runa** — you → you all   
**hay → haya** — he/she/it/... → they  

</details>  

### Possession

Put a personal pronoun directly before a noun:

`nim [noun]` = my [noun]  
`run [noun]` = your [noun]  
`hay [noun]` = his / her / their [noun]

## Vocabulary

### Building blocks

A lot of Oravia vocabulary is built from recurring sound-meaning pieces called **building blocks**.

Building blocks make vocabulary easier to learn and remember. When a piece turns up again, the old association comes with it. Many times, you may even get a sense of the word before learning it.

Because this is a short course written in English, most of the mnemonics here use English or familiar Romance-language connections. The Complete Course and the Building Blocks page use mnemonics and sound connections from languages around the world. 

**EO** — social relationships. *Mnemonic: **E**ach **O**ther.*  
**FA** — family. *Mnemonic: **family / familia / família**.*  
**BOE** — spaces in a house. *Mnemonic: English **bower**, a room/chamber.*  
**MO** — food, eating. *Mnemonic: **mmm** when food tastes good.*  

### Try boemo

**BOE** = a space/room in a house.  
**MO** = food.

What would you guess **boemo** means?

<details>
<summary>Reveal</summary>

**boemo** = **kitchen**.

</details>

### Words

| Oravia | Meaning | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| **eofa** | friend | **EO** social + **FA** family |
| **mo** | food; to eat | **MO** food/eating |
| **boemo** | kitchen; to cook | **BOE** house space + **MO** food/eating |
