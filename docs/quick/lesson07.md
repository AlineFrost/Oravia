# Lesson 7: Connecting Ideas

## Grammar

Four short connectors cover a lot of everyday speech:

**su** — and / with / also  
**mai** — but / however  
**dou** — or  
**eta** — so / therefore / then  

Did you know? In Italian, *ma*, which is similar to *mai*, also means *but*; and in Portuguese, *ou*, which is similar to *dou*, also means *or*. For *eta*, you may think of *ergo*, which came to English from *therefore* in Latin.  

`nim su eofa i anvu`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*A friend and I go / I go with a friend.*

</details>

`a wamu a yalgai, mai a leirih a yaltan`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*The fish is small, but the tree is big.*

</details>

`i mo dou i anefene?`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*Are you eating or resting?*

</details>

`nim i mo, eta i anefene`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I eat, then I rest.*

</details>

When two connected actions have the same obvious subject, you normally do not need to repeat the pronoun.

## Vocabulary

### SU also has a building-block meaning

You already know **su** as *and / with / also*. Its building-block meaning is **alongside**.

That gives you **fasu**:

**fasu** = **FA** family + **SU** alongside → sibling

Family members are expressed based on the generation. The sibling is the family member beside you in the same generation, that's why we say *alongside*.  

### Building blocks

**SU** — alongside. *Mnemonic: two things **side by side**.*  
**CO** — communication. *Mnemonic: **communication**.*  
**NI** — new. *Mnemonic: **new / neo**.*  
**RI** — act. *Mnemonic: a **rite** is an act.*  
**RO** — thinking, reasoning. *Mnemonic: **reason**.*  
**OI** — fun, amusement. *Mnemonic: **Oi!** as a lively exclamation.*  

### Words

| Oravia | Meaning | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| **fasu** | sibling | **FA** family + **SU** alongside |
| **leini** | seed | **LEI** plants + **NI** new |
| **eori** | polite, courteous; please | **EO** social + **RI** act |
| **anro** | decide | **AN** action + **RO** thinking |
| **oico** | gossip | **OI** fun + **CO** communication |

Look at two of the breakdowns:

**leini** = plant + new → **seed**  
**anro** = action + reasoning → **decide**
