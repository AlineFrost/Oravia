# Lesson 8: CA and Numbers 1–5

## Grammar

### CA connects a clause

Suppose you already have these two pieces:

`i ilian` — I know  
`run i anvu` — you go

Put **ca** before the second clause:

`i ilian ca run i anvu`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I know that you are going.*

</details>

The first **I** is left unstated because you are talking about yourself. **run** stays in the second clause because its subject is different.

Notice the pair:

**ce** asks for information.  
**ca** connects information.

!!! question "Spot the pair"
    You know **celi** = *when?* What would you expect **cali** to mean?

    <details>
    <summary>Reveal</summary>

    **cali** = **when** in a statement.

    </details>

One useful extension is **cali**:

**cali** = **CA + LI** time → *when*

`i anefene cali run i boemo`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I rest when you cook.*

</details>

The other CA forms follow the same pattern of changing *ce* to *ca*. You'll meet more of them later. **ca** and **cali** are already enough to build much longer sentences.

### Numbers 1–5

**tam** — 1  
**tor** — 2  
**par** — 3  
**bale** — 4  
**alui** — 5  

Nouns still do not change for plural:

`tam eofa` = one friend  
`par eofa` = three friends

## Vocabulary

### Building blocks

**ILA** — sharing information. *Mnemonic: “**I'lL Announce** it.”*  
**LUAN** — tongue. *Mnemonic: Spanish **lengua**, Portuguese **língua**, English **language**.*  
**MOA** — fruits. *Mnemonic: **M**ango, **O**range, **A**pple.*  
**LEN** — long. *Mnemonic: **length / longitude**.*  
**DAI** — desire, want. *Mnemonic: **desire**.*  

### Words

| Oravia | Meaning | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| **ilaluan** | say, tell | **ILA** sharing information + **LUAN** tongue |
| **moalen** | banana | **MOA** fruit + **LEN** long |
| **anidai** | want | **ANI** movement toward + **DAI** desire |
| **apavu** | run | **APA** physical movement + **VU** movement |

### Try ilidai

You already know **ILI** = cognition. This lesson gives you **DAI** = desire.

What does **ilidai** make you think of? We don't expect you to be able to guess new words correctly, but thinking about it may help you remember them. 

<details>
<summary>Reveal</summary>

**ilidai** = **intention**.

</details>

## Put it together

!!! tip "From earlier lessons"
    **ilian** = know  
    **anvu** = go  
    **leirih** = tree  
    **yaltan** = big  
    **eofa** = friend

`i ilian ca run i anvu`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I know that you are going.*

</details>

`i ilaluan ca a leirih a yaltan`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I say that the tree is big.*

</details>

`i anidai e par moalen, mai i mo e tam moalen`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I want three bananas, but I eat one banana.*

</details>

`i apavu cali eofa i anvu`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I run when a friend goes.*

</details>

`par eofa i anvu`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*Three friends go.*

</details>
