# Lesson 4: E Marker and the Copula

You can already say **i mo** “I eat.” But eat *what*?

## Grammar

### E marks what the action engages

Add **e** for the direct object.

You now have:

**A = Actor**  
**I = Initiates**  
**E = Engaging**

The basic pattern is:

`a [subject] i [verb] e [object]`

`nim i mo e mo`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*I eat food.*

</details>

The **e** tells you that the second **mo** — *food* — is the direct object.

### Saying “is” without a separate word for “is”

For ordinary identity and description, Oravia does not insert a separate *am / is / are* verb. Use **a** on both sides:

`a [subject] a [description or identity]`

Before looking at an example, try it yourself.

You already know **leirih** = *tree* and **yalgai** = *small*. How would you say **The tree is small**?

<details>
<summary>Reveal</summary>

`a leirih a yalgai`

*The tree is small.*

</details>

That's it: the second **a** introduces the identity or description.

### Objective and subjective qualities

Oravia separates two broad kinds of qualities:

**YAL** — measurable size/dimension: small, long, wide, and so on  
**YU** — subjective qualities: easy, difficult, pleasant, and so on

## Vocabulary

### Building blocks

**TAN** — big. *Mnemonic: a **Titan** is huge.*  
**ELE** — self-expansion, flourishing. *Mnemonic: **elevate**.*  
**YEL** — joining. *Mnemonic: think of a **yoke** joining two things.*  
**WA** — water, sea. *Mnemonic: **water**.*  
**MU** — small animals. *Mnemonic: a **mouse** is a small animal.*  

### Words

| Oravia | Meaning | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| **yaltan** | big, large | **YAL** size + **TAN** big |
| **eleyel** | love | **ELE** self-expansion + **YEL** joining |
| **wamu** | fish | **WA** water + **MU** small animal |
| **viwa** | blood | **VI** inside the body + **WA** water/liquid |
| **yugai** | easy | **YU** subjective quality + **GAI** small |
