# Lesson 6: Questions

## Grammar

### Yes/no questions: change the intonation

For a yes/no question, the words can stay the same. You may end the question with *ia* (yes), or *um* (no).

When you're speaking directly to someone, **run** is usually unnecessary:

`i anvu?`

<details class="example-translation">
<summary>Click to Reveal Translation</summary>

*Are you going?*

</details>

Notice this is the same as *i anvu, ia?*

### CE: “what?”

**ce** means *what?*, like *que* in Romance languages. For example:

`ce i mo? / e ce i mo?` means *what are you eating?*

The question word being in the beginning is not mandatory, as the order is flexible:

`i mo e ce?`

To form other question words, you take *ce* and then attach the kind of information you are asking for.

Try the pattern before seeing the little table.

**DOM** means *place*. What should **CE + DOM** ask?

<details>
<summary>Reveal</summary>

**cedom** = **where?**

</details>

Now another one:

**LI** means *time*. What should **CE + LI** ask?

<details>
<summary>Reveal</summary>

**celi** = **when?**

</details>

The core set is:

| Form | Built from | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **ce** | — | what? |
| **cei** | **CE + EI** person | who? |
| **cedom** | **CE + DOM** place | where? |
| **celi** | **CE + LI** time | when? |

These four cover most of what you'll need here. The rest of the question family follows the same pattern.

## Vocabulary

### Building blocks

**DOM** — place. *Mnemonic: **domicile**; Latin/Romance **domus**.*  
**LI** — time. *Mnemonic: **lifetime / timeline**.*  
**NON** — path, way, manner. *Mnemonic: moving **on and on** along a path.*  
**GE** — finances. *Mnemonic: **get / give** money.*  
**ILI** — cognition. *Mnemonic: **illuminate** an idea; **intelligence**.*  
**FOU** — lack. *Mnemonic: **few** — you need something when you have too little of it.*  

### Words

| Oravia | Meaning | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| **gedom** | bank | **GE** finances + **DOM** place |
| **wanon** | swim | **WA** water + **NON** path |
| **ilian** | know | **ILI** cognition + **AN** action |
| **anifou** | need | **ANI** movement toward + **FOU** lack |
