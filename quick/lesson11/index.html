    Lesson 11: Compounds, Hyphens, and Imports

    What if the word you need isn't in the dictionary?

    Grammar

    So far you've mostly worked inside words: BOE + MO → boemo. You can also combine whole words.

    Ordinary modifier: describe this particular thing

    yoyar beivu

    Click to Reveal Translation *a white car*

    Here yoyar just describes this car.

    Compound: make one concept

    To indicate that the words together create a category or concept, we add -s to the modifier. We may also add sol before the compound to make it extra explicit, but that's optional.

    Now imagine a vehicle that is basically a kitchen on wheels:

    sol boemos beivu

    Click to Reveal Translation *kitchen-vehicle → a food truck*

    The -s marks compound words except for the last. sol announces to the listener that a compound is coming.

    You can still describe that food truck:

    yoyar sol boemos beivu

    Click to Reveal Translation *a white food truck*

    yoyar describes this particular one. sol boemos beivu names the kind of vehicle.

    Hyphen: blend two meanings

    With a hyphen, one word is coloring the meaning of another. It is usually done with verbs to give them more nuance and expression, and make sentences more direct:

    i eori-ilaluan u eofa

    Click to Reveal Translation *I speak politely / courteously to a friend.*

    Here ilaluan is still the main action, and eori gives it the polite flavor.

    Imports and local coinages

    A word outside the official vocabulary begins with an apostrophe:

    'Italia — Italy
    'Nihongo — Japanese language

    Use the apostrophe for names and other words brought in from outside the fixed Oravia vocabulary: places, dishes, languages, species, local coinages, and so on.

    Vocabulary

    Before you split a word apart

    A root can be more than one syllable. FENE, LUAN, PILI, and REVA are each single roots, so they don't split further. The same letters may also do different jobs in different words. There are only about ~260 building blocks, and once learned, recognizing them becomes easy.

    Building blocks

    CIU — creative products. Mnemonic: create.
    REVA — accurate, real. Mnemonic: real / verify.
    MIR — lots, much, many. Mnemonic: myriad.
    SOI — speed. Mnemonic: Sprint Out Immediately.
    VIO — change. Mnemonic: variation.
    PILI — delicate. Mnemonic: picture a delicate petal.

    Words

    Oravia Meaning Breakdown
    ciureva photo, picture CIU creative product + REVA accurate/real
    lumirwa flood LU outside/weather + MIR lots + WA water
    lisoi soon LI time + SOI speed
    leipili flower LEI plants + PILI delicate

    Six words you were never taught

    You've learned enough building blocks to try six dictionary words that never appeared in the lessons. See what you make of them before clicking.

    1. wilgai

    WIL human-made geography + GAI small → wilgai = ?

    Reveal **village / town**

    2. visro

    VIS organs + RO thinking → visro = ?

    Reveal **brain**

    3. lealei

    LEA landscape + LEI plants → lealei = ?

    Reveal **forest**

    4. wilyel

    WIL human-made geography + YEL joining → wilyel = ?

    Reveal **bridge**

    5. apasoi

    APA physical movement + SOI speed → apasoi = ?

    Reveal **hurry / rush**

    6. oipili

    OI fun + PILI delicate → oipili = ?

    Reveal **cute / adorable**

    With the building blocks from this short course, more than 40 additional dictionary words have breakdowns made entirely from pieces you already know.

    Put it together

    From earlier lessons

    leayo = garden
    eori = polite / please
    ilaluan = say / tell
    eofa = friend
    lis = one day / sometime in the future

    i anona e ciureva u eofa

    Click to Reveal Translation *I give a photo to a friend.*

    a lumirwa a yaltan

    Click to Reveal Translation *The flood is big.*

    lisoi, i anvu en leayo

    Click to Reveal Translation *Soon, I go to the garden.*

    a leipili en leayo

    Click to Reveal Translation *The flower is in the garden.*

    sol boemos beivu

    Click to Reveal Translation *kitchen-vehicle → food truck*

    i eori-ilaluan u eofa

    Click to Reveal Translation *I speak politely to a friend.*

    lis, i anvu en 'Italia

    Click to Reveal Translation *One day, I will go to Italy.*
